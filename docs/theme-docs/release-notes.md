# Release notes

## 1.0.0

The first release of Caliper.

**Spec sheet**

- Catalog numbers on product cards, the product page, the breadcrumb and cart lines, from a product metafield.
- Dimension lines drawn on the edges of product images, product cards and the Catalog hero, from width and height metafields.
- Option codes, such as F-01 or M-02, in the variant picker, the index and the cart.
- Gallery images numbered as figures.
- A Specification block with up to eight rows connected to metafields.
- Collections, search results and the home page as an index table, with columns chosen per section and a switch between index and plates.

**Presets**

- **Caliper** for furniture: framed product cards with dimension lines, thumbnails below the gallery, fabric codes, lead times per product.
- **Seam** for clothing: portrait product cards with sizes, a two-column gallery, color codes, fit notes and a size guide drawer.
- **Facet** for jewelry: a dark color scheme, square product cards with materials, thumbnails beside the gallery, ring sizes in rows, an engraving field and italic accents in headings.

**Product pages**

- Blocks for title, price, Shop Pay Installments, variant picker, fit notes, dimensions, specification, engraving, lead time, buy buttons, pickup availability, collapsible tabs, text and custom Liquid.
- Variant picker as buttons, a dropdown or rows with codes and prices.
- Color and pattern swatches from Shopify's category metafields.
- Size guide drawer linked next to chosen options.
- Sticky add to cart bar on mobile.
- Gift card recipient form.
- Related and complementary product recommendations.

**Collections and search**

- Filters in a bar, sidebar or drawer, and sorting, powered by the Search and Discovery app.
- Product cards with sizes, material or dimensions, color swatches and a second image on hover.
- Predictive search with products, collections, pages and articles.
- Page numbers or a load more button.

**Cart**

- Cart drawer or cart page as an order: numbered lines with catalog numbers, option codes and lead times, plus cart notes, gift wrapping and a size reminder.

**Sections**

- Catalog hero and Product index for the home page, and more than 30 other sections, including shoppable image, collection tabs, fabric library, material guide, gift guide, delivery and assembly, size guide, product comparison, lookbook and testimonials.

**Design and accessibility**

- Color schemes, three fonts from Shopify's library (headings, body and labels), two header layouts with a numbered menu, three corner styles.
- Animation speed setting and section reveal on scroll, both off for visitors who ask for reduced motion.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, focus kept inside open drawers and touch targets of at least 44 × 44px on mobile.
