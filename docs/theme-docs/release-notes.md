# Release notes

## 1.0.0

The first release of Arvel.

**Presets**

- **Arvel** for apparel: portrait product cards with sizes, a two-column product gallery, fit notes and a size guide drawer.
- **Facet** for jewelry: square product cards with materials, thumbnails beside the gallery, an engraving field and gift wrapping.
- **Sotto** for furniture: framed product cards with dimensions, thumbnails below the gallery, a dimensions block and lead times.

**Product pages**

- Blocks for title, price, Shop Pay Installments, variant picker, fit notes, dimensions, engraving, lead time, buy buttons, pickup availability, collapsible tabs, text and custom Liquid.
- Color and pattern swatches from Shopify's category metafields.
- Size guide drawer linked next to chosen options.
- Sticky add to cart bar on mobile.
- Gift card recipient form.
- Related and complementary product recommendations.

**Collections and search**

- Filters in a bar, sidebar or drawer, and sorting, powered by the Search and Discovery app.
- Product cards with sizes, material or dimensions, color swatches and a second image on hover.
- Predictive search with products, collections, pages and articles.
- Page numbers or a load more button.

**Cart**

- Cart drawer or cart page, with cart notes, gift wrapping, a size reminder and lead times on each line.

**Sections**

- More than 30 sections, including image banner, slideshow, shoppable image, collection tabs, gift guide, material guide, fabric library, craft process, delivery and assembly, size guide, product comparison, lookbook and testimonials.

**Design and accessibility**

- Color schemes, Shopify fonts, three corner styles and three header layouts.
- Animation speed setting and section reveal on scroll, both off for visitors who ask for reduced motion.
- Keyboard navigation, visible focus, focus kept inside open drawers and touch targets of at least 44 × 44px on mobile.
