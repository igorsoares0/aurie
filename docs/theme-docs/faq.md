# FAQ

## Setup

**Why don't I see the demo store's photos after installing?**
Demo images and products don't transfer when you install a theme. Image areas show placeholders until you add your own images, and product sections show your store's products.

**How do I change from one preset to another?**
In the theme editor, open **Theme settings**, select **Change theme style** and pick another preset. See [Presets](presets.md).

**How do I split a heading into two halves?**
Type `|` with a space on each side between the two halves, such as `Shop | by room`. Turn on **Theme settings › Typography › Show rule in split headings** to draw a line between them.

**Can I use my own fonts?**
Arvel uses Shopify's font library, which loads fonts quickly and in the right weights. Pick them in **Theme settings › Typography**.

## Products

**How do I add color swatches?**
Swatches come from your product's category metafields. See [Metafields and swatches](metafields-and-swatches.md).

**Why does a product card show no sizes?**
**Card details** must be set to **Sizes**, and the product's size option must be named as in **Size option names**, such as `Size`. Add other names, such as `Waist`, separated by commas.

**How do I add a size guide?**
Create a page with your size table, then choose it as the **Size guide page** in the product page's **Variant picker** block. See [Product pages](product-pages.md#size-guide).

**How do I show a product's dimensions or material?**
Create a product metafield and connect it in **Theme settings › Product cards** or in a product block. See [Metafields and swatches](metafields-and-swatches.md).

**Where do complementary products come from?**
From the Search & Discovery app. Until you add complementary products for a product, that section stays hidden.

## Collections and search

**How do I choose which filters appear?**
Install the free Search & Discovery app and add your filters under **Filters**. See [Collections and search](collections-and-search.md).

**Can filters appear in a sidebar?**
Yes. In the **Product grid** section, set **Filter layout on desktop** to **Sidebar**. Mobile always uses a drawer.

## Cart and checkout

**How do I offer gift wrapping?**
In the **Cart** section of the cart page, turn on **Show gift wrapping option**. The customer's choice is saved with the order. See [Cart](cart.md).

**Can I send customers straight to the cart page instead of the drawer?**
Yes. Set **Theme settings › Cart › Cart type** to **Page**.

## Languages and markets

**How do I show a country or language selector?**
Add markets and languages in **Settings › Markets** and **Settings › Languages**. Then turn on **Show country/region selector** and **Show language selector** in the **Footer** section, and in the **Header** section for the mobile menu.

## Accessibility and performance

**Is the theme accessible?**
Arvel is built for keyboard and screen reader use: focus is always visible, drawers trap focus and close with Escape, and touch targets are at least 44 × 44 px on mobile. Keep enough contrast between the background and text colors in your color schemes.

**Can I turn off animations?**
Yes. Set **Theme settings › Animations › Speed** to **None**. Visitors who ask their device to reduce motion never see animations.
