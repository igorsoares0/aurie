# Arvel theme documentation

Arvel is a Shopify theme for brands that sell considered goods: clothing, jewelry and furniture. It comes with three presets, each a complete starting point with its own type, colors, product cards and product page:

| Preset | Made for | Look and feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Arvel** | Fashion and apparel | Editorial serif headings, portrait product cards, a two-column product gallery, square corners |
| **Facet** | Jewelry and accessories | Refined serif with italic accents, square product cards, thumbnails beside the gallery, pill buttons |
| **Sotto** | Furniture and home | Condensed uppercase headings, framed product cards with dimensions, thumbnails below the gallery, rounded corners |

All three presets share the same sections and blocks, so you can switch presets or mix their ideas at any time.
