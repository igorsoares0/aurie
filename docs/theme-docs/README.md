# Caliper theme documentation

Caliper is a Shopify theme for brands that make things carefully and want to say exactly what they sell. It treats the store as a technical catalog: every product carries a catalog number, its measurements are drawn as dimension lines on the photo, its options have codes, and its details sit in a spec sheet. Collections can be read as plates or as an index table.

It comes with three presets, each a complete starting point with its own type, colors, product cards and product page:

| Preset | Made for | Look and feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Caliper** | Furniture and home | Warm paper and ink, a grotesque heading font, framed product cards with dimension lines, thumbnails below the gallery |
| **Seam** | Clothing | Cool blueprint paper with navy ink and a blue accent, portrait product cards with sizes, a two-column gallery |
| **Facet** | Jewelry | Black paper with cream text and gold, a serif with italic accents, square product cards, thumbnails beside the gallery |

All three presets share the same sections and blocks, so you can switch presets or mix their ideas at any time.
