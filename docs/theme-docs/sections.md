# Sections

Add a section from **Add section** in any template. Every section has a **Color scheme** setting; a scheme other than the page's own gives the section a filled background.

Most sections that hold text accept a split heading: type `|` with a space on each side between the two halves, such as `Shop | by room`.

## Images and banners

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Image banner** | A large image with a heading, text and up to two buttons. Choose a **Height** (Small, Medium, Large or Full screen), **Inset with rounded corners**, an **Overlay opacity**, a **Content position** and a **Content layout** (Stacked or In one line). Add a **Mobile image** for a crop made for phones. On phones, text sits at the foot of the image over a soft fade. |
| **Slideshow** | Several **Slide** blocks, each with an image, heading, text and button. Turn on **Change slides automatically** and set **Change slides every**; a pause button lets visitors stop the rotation. |
| **Image with text** | An image beside **Caption**, **Heading**, **Text** and **Button** blocks. **Layout** puts the image first or second, **Width** is **Full width** or **Page width**, and **Tag on image** adds a small label over the photo. |
| **Video** | A video from your files or a YouTube or Vimeo link (**Video link**), with a **Cover image** that starts the video when selected. Add a **Video description** for screen readers. |
| **Lookbook** | A scrolling row of **Look** blocks, each with an image, a heading and a link. |
| **Detail gallery** | Close-up images, each with a short caption. |

## Products and collections

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Featured collection** | Products from one collection. **Layout** is **Scrolling row** or **Grid**; **Show product count** adds the number next to the heading. |
| **Collection tabs** | Several collections behind tabs. Add one **Tab** block per collection. |
| **Collection list** | Collection tiles. **Style** is **Numbered index**, **Portraits with caption** or **Mosaic**. Add **Collection** blocks, or **Link** blocks for pages such as a fit guide. |
| **Featured product** | One product with its gallery, price, variant selector and add to cart button. |
| **Shoppable image** | An image with hotspots. Each **Product hotspot** block points to a product, with its **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position** on the image. **Layout** is **Image with product list** (shoppers pick a variant for each piece and add the whole look at once) or **Image with floating card**. |
| **Shop by room** | **Room** tiles, each with an image, heading, subtitle and link. |
| **Product comparison** | Up to four rows compared across products. Each **Row metafield** reads a product metafield, such as `custom.width`. |
| **Gift guide** | Tabs of gift ideas by **Occasion**, such as birthdays or anniversaries, each showing a collection and an image. |
| **Product recommendations** | Used on the product page. **Recommendation type** is **Related products** or **Complementary products**. Complementary products are set in the Search & Discovery app; the section stays hidden until there are some. |

## Brand and editorial

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rich text** | A caption, heading, text and button. **Layout** is **Centered**, **Left** or **Split heading**, which places the **Heading** and the **Second half of heading** at opposite sides of the page. |
| **Multicolumn** | **Column** blocks with an image or icon, a heading, text and a link. **Style** is **Images with caption**, **Icons, centered** or **Icons beside text**. |
| **Testimonials** | Customer quotes shown one at a time, with arrows. |
| **Logo list** | Press or partner logos. |
| **Blog posts** | The latest posts from a blog. |
| **Email signup** | A newsletter signup form. |
| **Collapsible content** | Questions and answers, or any text in rows that open and close. |

## Materials and service

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Material guide** | Your materials side by side, each with an image, a name and care notes. |
| **Fabric library** | **Fabric** blocks with an image or a **Swatch color**, a **Name** and a **Type**, and an optional material panel with a heading, text and points. |
| **Craft process** | Numbered steps of how your products are made. |
| **Delivery and assembly** | The stages from order to delivery. |
| **Size guide** | A size table from a page (**Size table page**) with **Measuring tip** blocks. |

## Utility

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Custom Liquid** | Your own Liquid, HTML or embed code. |
| **Apps** | Blocks from apps you install, anywhere on a page. App blocks can also be added inside the product, collection, search results, cart, blog, article, page, featured product, contact form and footer sections. |
| **Contact form** | A contact form, used on the contact page. |
