# Theme settings

Open the theme editor and select the gear icon (**Theme settings**). These settings apply to the whole store.

## Colors

Arvel uses **color schemes**: named sets of colors that you assign to sections. Each preset comes with a light scheme and a dark one, and you can add more.

Each scheme has these colors:

| Color | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Background | The section background |
| Background gradient | Replaces the solid background where gradients are supported |
| Text | Headings and body text |
| Secondary text | Captions, labels, notes and compare-at prices |
| Image background | The tile behind product photos and placeholders |
| Divider | Hairlines between rows and blocks |
| Border | Field and outline button borders |
| Accent | Links on hover, sale prices, sale badges and discounts |
| Text on accent | Text placed on an accent background, such as the fabric library's highlight |
| Solid button background | Solid buttons |
| Solid button label | Text on solid buttons |

Keep enough contrast between each background and its text color. The theme doesn't change your colors to fix contrast.

## Typography

- **Heading font** and **Body font**: chosen from Shopify's font library. Bold and italic styles are loaded automatically.
- **Heading size**: scales every heading, from 80% to 150%.
- **Heading case**: **Default** or **Uppercase**.
- **Show rule in split headings**: adds a line between the two halves of a split heading. To split a heading, type `|` with a space on each side between the halves, such as `Shop | by room`.

## Layout

- **Page width**: **Narrow** or **Wide**.
- **Page margin**: the space at the left and right of the page on desktop. Mobile always uses 16px.
- **Space between sections**: the vertical space between sections on desktop. Mobile uses a smaller value.
- **Grid spacing**: the gap between product cards and images: **Tight**, **Medium** or **Airy**.

## Shape

- **Corner style**: **Square**, **Rounded** (rounded buttons, fields and images) or **Pill buttons** (fully rounded buttons and fields, square images).

## Animations

- **Speed**: how fast drawers, menus and collapsible rows open and close: **None**, **Quick**, **Balanced** or **Slow**.
- **Reveal sections on scroll**: sections below the first screen fade in once as visitors scroll. It has no effect when the speed is **None**.

Visitors who ask their device to reduce motion never see animations, whatever these settings say.

## Header

- **Layout**: **Centered logo, text links**; **Centered logo, icons, menu below**; or **Logo on the left, centered menu**. See [Header and footer](header-and-footer.md).

## Product cards

- **Card style**: **Portrait** (tall images, details below), **Square** (square images, centered details) or **Framed** (each card on its own tile).
- **Image ratio**: **Match card style**, **Portrait**, **Square** or **Landscape**.
- **Card details**: an extra line under the product name: **None**, **Sizes**, **Material** or **Dimensions**.
- **Size option names**: the option names treated as sizes, separated by commas, such as `Size, Waist`.
- **Material metafield** and **Dimensions metafield**: the product metafields shown when **Card details** is **Material** or **Dimensions**. See [Metafields and swatches](metafields-and-swatches.md).
- **Show second image on hover**: shows the product's second image when the pointer is over the card.

## Product media

- **Gallery layout**: **Grid** (two columns), **Thumbnails beside** or **Thumbnails below**. On mobile every layout becomes a swipeable gallery.

## Cart

- **Cart type**: **Drawer** (opens over the page) or **Page**.
- **Show cart note**: lets customers leave a note with the order.
- **Show size reminder**: shows a note when the cart has the same product in two sizes.
- **Lead time metafield**: a product metafield shown next to each cart line, such as a delivery estimate.

See [Cart](cart.md).

## Social media

Add the full address of each account: **Instagram**, **Pinterest**, **TikTok**, **Facebook**, **YouTube** and **X**. The links appear in the footer's **Social media** block.

## Favicon

- **Favicon image**: shown in the browser tab at 32 × 32px. Use a square image.
