# Cart

## Cart drawer or cart page

In **Theme settings › Cart › Cart type**:

- **Drawer**: adding a product opens a panel over the current page, with the cart lines, totals and the checkout button. Customers can change quantities or remove items without leaving the page. A **View cart** link opens the full cart page.
- **Page**: adding a product goes to the cart page.

Both show the subtotal, automatic discounts, a note about taxes and shipping, the checkout button and the accelerated checkout buttons your store offers.

## Cart note

Turn on **Theme settings › Cart › Show cart note** to let customers leave a note with the order. Set the **Note label** and **Note placeholder** in the **Cart drawer** section and in the **Cart** section of the cart page.

## Lead time on cart lines

Each cart line can show a short product-specific line, such as "Made to order, ships in 6–8 weeks". Store it in a product metafield and enter its namespace and key in **Theme settings › Cart › Lead time metafield** (for example `custom.lead_time`).

## Cart page options

These options are in the **Cart** section of the cart page:

**Gift wrapping**

- **Show gift wrapping option** adds a checkbox to the cart.
- **Option label** and **Option note** set its text, such as "Wrap each piece separately" and "Free".
- **Order detail name** is the name shown with the order in your admin when the customer selects it.

**Size reminder**

When a customer has the same product in two sizes, the cart page shows a reminder to check the fit. Turn it on in **Theme settings › Cart › Show size reminder** and choose the **Size guide page** to link to.

**Empty cart**

Set the **Heading**, **Text**, **Button label** and **Link** shown when the cart is empty.
