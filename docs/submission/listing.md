### One-line summary

An editorial theme for brands that sell considered goods, with presets for apparel, jewelry and furniture.

### Theme description

Arvel is built for small brands that sell carefully made products and want their store to read like a printed catalog. Large photography, quiet typography and generous space put the products first, while the details shoppers need sit close at hand: sizes and fit notes for clothing, materials and engraving for jewelry, dimensions and lead times for furniture.

Each preset is a complete starting point with its own type, colors, product cards and product page. All three share the same sections and blocks, so a store can grow without changing themes.

## Preset: Arvel

- **Primary industry:** Clothing and fashion
- **Catalog size:** Small
- **Demo store:** https://arvel-theme-9h3bmm3m.myshopify.com

**Summary:** An editorial preset for apparel brands, with portrait product cards and a fit-first product page.

**Description:** Arvel pairs a classic serif with clean sans-serif text on warm paper tones. Portrait product cards show every size at a glance, with sold-out sizes struck through. The product page opens with a two-column gallery and keeps fit notes, a size guide drawer and material care within reach. A shoppable look on the home page lets shoppers pick a size for each piece and add the whole outfit at once.

**Key features:**

- Portrait product cards that list sizes and mark sold-out sizes
- Size guide in a drawer, linked next to the size option
- Fit notes on the product page, from a product metafield
- Shoppable look: add every piece of an outfit to the cart at once
- Size reminder in the cart when the same product is in two sizes
- Filters in a bar above the grid, or in a sidebar or drawer

## Preset: Facet

- **Primary industry:** Jewelry and accessories
- **Catalog size:** Small
- **Demo store:** https://arvel-facet-7rk7w1ea.myshopify.com

**Summary:** A refined preset for jewelry brands, with square product cards and a gallery made for detail.

**Description:** Facet sets a high-contrast serif with italic accents against near-white backgrounds. Square product cards show the metal or material under each name, and swatches switch the photo to the chosen finish. The product page places thumbnails beside a large image for close looks, collects engraving text with a character limit and opens a ring size guide in a drawer. Gift wrapping can be chosen in the cart.

**Key features:**

- Square product cards with the material under each name
- Metal and finish swatches on product cards and product pages
- Engraving field with a character limit, saved with the order
- Gift guide section with tabs by occasion
- Material guide section for metals and care
- Gift wrapping option in the cart

## Preset: Sotto

- **Primary industry:** Furniture
- **Catalog size:** Small
- **Demo store:** https://arvel-sotto-iivcamps.myshopify.com

**Summary:** A calm preset for furniture and home brands, with framed product cards that show dimensions.

**Description:** Sotto uses condensed uppercase headings, soft linen and walnut tones and rounded corners. Framed product cards show each piece's dimensions, and a dimensions block on the product page lists width, depth, height and seat height. Lead times appear on the product page and in the cart, so shoppers know when made-to-order pieces arrive. Sections for rooms, fabrics, the craft process and delivery and assembly tell the story behind each piece.

**Key features:**

- Framed product cards with dimensions under each name
- Dimensions block with up to four measurements from metafields
- Lead time on the product page and on each cart line
- Shop by room and collection tabs on the home page
- Fabric library with swatches, names and material notes
- Delivery and assembly section that walks through each stage
