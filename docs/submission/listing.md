### One-line summary

An editorial theme for brands that sell considered goods, with presets for apparel, jewelry and furniture.

### Theme description

Arvel is built for small brands that sell carefully made products and want their store to read like a printed catalog. Large photography, quiet typography and generous space put the products first, while the details shoppers need sit close at hand: sizes and fit notes for clothing, materials and engraving for jewelry, dimensions and lead times for furniture.

Each preset is a complete starting point with its own type, colors, product cards and product page. All three share the same sections and blocks, so a store can grow without changing themes.

## Other notes (as submitted)

**What makes your theme unique from others?**

Arvel is built from Shopify's Skeleton theme with original code, around one idea: each industry needs different product information in front of the shopper, not just a different look. The three presets share one codebase but change the structure of the store, not only colors and fonts.

Product cards change what they show: every size with sold-out sizes struck through for clothing, the metal or material for jewelry, and measurements in a framed card for furniture. The product gallery changes layout per preset (a two-column grid, thumbnails beside a large image, or thumbnails below), and so do the header and corner styles.

Each preset also has its own working features: a shoppable look where shoppers choose a size for each piece and add the whole outfit at once, a cart reminder when the same item is in two sizes, an engraving field with a character limit saved to the order, a gift guide by occasion, a material guide, a fabric library, a dimensions block, and lead times shown on the product page and on each cart line.

**What's your past experience with developing themes?**

Arvel is our first theme for the Theme Store. We build and maintain public Shopify apps, so we work with Liquid, theme app extensions and the Admin API regularly. For Arvel we followed the Theme Store requirements throughout: Theme Check passes with no offenses, the three demo stores average 79 to 91 on mobile Lighthouse performance and 98 or higher on desktop, accessibility scores 100 on every tested page, and keyboard navigation, focus management and touch target sizes were tested on each preset.
