## Refund policy

We want you to love every piece. If something isn't right, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

**What can be returned**
- Unworn pieces in their original condition, with the linen box and card.
- Earrings can be returned only if their seal is unbroken, for hygiene reasons.
- Engraved and resized pieces are made for you and are final sale, unless they arrive damaged or with a mistake on our side.

**How to start a return**
Write to us through the contact page with your order number. We'll send a prepaid return label. Returns within the United States are free.

**Refunds**
Once your return reaches the studio, we inspect it within 3 business days and refund the original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit. Gift cards are refunded as store credit.

**Exchanges**
The fastest way to change a size or metal is to return the piece and place a new order. Ring resizing is free within 30 days on solid gold and sterling silver.

**Damaged or wrong items**
If a piece arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we'll make it right at no cost.

## Shipping policy

**Processing time**
In-stock pieces leave the studio within 1–2 business days. Engraved pieces ship in 5 business days.

**Shipping in the United States**
Free standard shipping on every order, delivered in 2–4 business days. Express delivery (1–2 business days) is available at checkout.

**International shipping**
We ship to Canada and selected countries. Rates and delivery times are shown at checkout. Duties and taxes may apply on delivery and are the customer's responsibility.

**Packaging**
Every order arrives in our linen gift box with a care cloth and a card. Gift orders never include prices.

**Tracking**
You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

**Lost or delayed parcels**
If your order hasn't arrived within 10 business days of shipping, contact us and we'll trace it or send a replacement.

## Privacy policy

This policy explains how Oriel ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares your personal information when you visit or buy from our online store.

**Information we collect**
- Details you give us: name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number, order details and any engraving text.
- Payment information, processed securely by our payment providers. We never store full card numbers.
- Information collected automatically: device and browser information, pages viewed and how you interact with the store, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

**How we use it**
- To process and deliver your orders, including engraving and returns.
- To answer questions and provide customer support.
- To send newsletters, only if you sign up. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To keep the store secure and improve how it works.

**Sharing**
We share information only with service providers that help us run the store, such as our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment processors and shipping carriers, and when required by law. We don't sell your personal information.

**Your rights**
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, and to object to some uses. To make a request, contact us through the contact page.

**Retention**
We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes, and other information only as long as it is useful to you.

**Changes**
We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
