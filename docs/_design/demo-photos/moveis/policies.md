## Refund policy

Live with your piece for 30 nights. If it isn't right for your home, tell us within 30 nights of delivery and we'll collect it for free and refund you in full.

**What can be returned**
- Sofas, armchairs, beds, tables and storage in their delivered condition, within 30 nights of delivery.
- Lighting, mirrors and textiles that are unused and in their original packaging.
- Custom-sized pieces and textiles that have been used can't be returned.

**How to start a return**
Write to us through the contact page with your order number. We'll call to agree a collection slot. Our team takes the piece apart and carries it away.

**Refunds**
Once the piece is back in our workshop, we inspect it within 5 business days and refund the original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit. Gift cards are refunded as store credit.

**Damaged or faulty pieces**
If a piece arrives damaged, tell our delivery team or contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We'll repair or replace it at no cost. Defects that appear later are covered by our warranty: 10 years on sofa, bed and table frames, and 2 years on fabrics and finishes.

## Shipping policy

**Lead times**
Every piece is made to order in our workshop and ships in 6 to 8 weeks. Pieces marked In stock ship within 5 business days. The expected lead time is shown on each product page and in your cart.

**Delivery and assembly**
When your piece is ready, we call to agree a two-hour delivery slot. Two of our team carry it to the room you choose, assemble it and take every bit of packaging away.

**Cost**
Delivery and assembly is free on orders over $1,500 in the continental United States. Below that, it's a flat $95. Lighting, mirrors and textiles ship by courier for free.

**Where we deliver**
We deliver to the continental United States. For other addresses, contact us before ordering.

**Before we arrive**
Measure doorways, stairs and corners against the dimensions on the product page. Sofas and beds arrive with their legs off, so the depth is the number that matters most. If a piece can't reach the room safely, we'll take it back and refund you in full.

**Fabric samples**
Samples are free and posted within two business days.

## Privacy policy

This policy explains how Sotto ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares your personal information when you visit or buy from our online store.

**Information we collect**
- Details you give us: name, email, delivery and billing address, phone number and order details.
- Payment information, processed securely by our payment providers. We never store full card numbers.
- Information collected automatically: device and browser information, pages viewed and how you interact with the store, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

**How we use it**
- To make, deliver and assemble your order, and to arrange collections and returns.
- To call you about delivery slots.
- To send newsletters and workshop notes, only if you sign up. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To keep the store secure and improve how it works.

**Sharing**
We share information only with service providers that help us run the store, such as our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment processors and delivery partners, and when required by law. We don't sell your personal information.

**Your rights**
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, and to object to some uses. To make a request, contact us through the contact page.

**Retention**
We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, warranty and legal purposes, and other information only as long as it is useful to you.

**Changes**
We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
